# How to stop writing quotes by hand in a small business in Mallorca

<div className="mt-6 border-l-2 border-coral pl-5 text-[18px] leading-relaxed text-[#F4F6FF]">

  straight away. Which one you need comes down to a single question: how many pricing rules you
  have, and how many times a day you apply them.
</div>

## Why one quote eats a whole morning

Picture an installation company in Palma asked to quote for fitting a unit. Someone has to check
the measurements, look up prices, work out materials, add travel, apply the margin and put
together a document the customer can read.

Then that quote goes out by email or WhatsApp and sits there. If the customer asks for a change,
recalculate. If they accept, someone copies the data into the management software, creates the
record and schedules the work.

Each step looks small. The problem shows up when it happens four times a day.

You do not need to be a large company to have this problem. It is enough to have several rate
cards, configurable products or a sale that depends on a lot of manual arithmetic. In
[renovations](/sectores/reformas), joinery and installation work it is close to universal.

## The three ways to automate it, from least to most

The right answer depends on how each business works, and often nothing new needs building: it
can be enough to tidy up the spreadsheet you already have or connect two tools you already pay
for. When the process is more involved, there are three routes.

### 1. A quote template that does the maths

If all your quotes follow roughly the same structure, a well-built template applies the rates,
calculates the totals and produces the document without repeating the steps by hand.

It is the sensible option when there are not many pricing rules and what is missing is order,
not software. It costs little and you feel it the same week.

### 2. Connecting quotes to your management software

The next step up is for the quote to talk to your [CRM](/modulos), your stock or your invoicing.
Customer details get typed once. When a quote is accepted and turns into a job, the information
moves on by itself instead of starting again from the beginning.

### 3. A quote configurator on your website

If your customers choose sizes, materials, finishes, quantities or extras, a configurator on
your site lets them build what they want and see a figure immediately.

A joinery in Mallorca, for instance, can let the customer pick the dimensions of a unit, the
timber and the finish. The system applies your pricing rules, gives the number and leaves you
the details you need to carry on the sale. Depending on how complex it is, it can produce a
guide price or a full proposal waiting for you to approve it.

<Nota>
  Automating a calculation does **not** mean every quote goes out unseen. Unusual jobs, costs
  that move and awkward conditions still need a person to sign them off. A well-built
  configurator knows when to stop and hand the decision back to you.
</Nota>

## When it is not worth it

If you write three quotes a week and each one is unlike the last, automating it means spending
money to solve something that is not costing you money. The same goes if your price comes out of
visiting the site: what gets automated there is the paperwork afterwards, not the calculation.

We are not saying this out of modesty. We are saying it because it is the mistake we see most
often: [automating before anyone looked at the
process](/blog/por-que-diagnosticar-antes-de-automatizar) does not fix it, it speeds it up.

## What we can do

At Nexo4Pymes we build [custom solutions](/servicios) around the processes a business actually
has. In this area that usually means:

- A web configurator that calculates with your rates and your conditions.
- Customers, quotes and sales history in one place.
- The website form connected to your management software.
- Documents generated automatically, without typing the same data twice.
- Follow-up on whatever is waiting for an answer.
- Integrating what you already use when it makes sense, rather than replacing it on principle.

## What it costs

There is no single price, and be wary of anyone who gives you one without asking anything. It
depends on how many pricing rules there are, how many products or services, what has to be
connected and how far you want to take it. A template and a configurator wired into stock and
sales are nowhere near the same job.

That is why we always start the same way: a free fifteen-minute video call and, if it is
warranted, a diagnosis that puts in writing what is worth improving, how far it goes and in what
order. If the answer is that an off-the-shelf tool will do, we tell you that too.
